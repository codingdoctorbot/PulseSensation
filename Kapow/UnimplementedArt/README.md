# Unimplemented art

69 user-reviewed messages, 74 three-frame sequences including five alternate orange versions (222 TGA files).

Files: `<category>/<message>_1.tga` through `_3.tga`. Frame order: onset, peak, aftershock. Each is an uncompressed 32-bit RGBA TGA with alpha. Dimensions: 256x256, 512x512; see each asset's frame_size in inventory (default256x256).

The five original orange alternatives are in `original-orange/escalation/`, with identical logical filenames. TRIPLE KILL, MEGA KILL and KILLING SPREE already retain their original orange art in the primary escalation folder. All originals also remain intact in their source batches.

These files are packaged for future integration. They are not registered in ArtCatalog, referenced by the TOC, or connected to events/triggers.

`inventory.json` records selected source batches, original PNG/prompt manifest paths, and copied-file hashes. High-resolution PNGs and comparison sheets stay in `Art/_batches/` at repository root.

Implementation handoff: [handoff.md](handoff.md) records final asset status and provenance; [implementation.md](implementation.md) covers catalog import, event/theme mapping, animation, profiles, the VS portrait concept, validation and optional future brainstorming.
